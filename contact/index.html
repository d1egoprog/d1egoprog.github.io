About my work

I research Knowledge Graphs and Neuro-Symbolic AI at the Institute for Data, Process and Knowledge Management at WU Vienna. My work focuses on knowledge graph construction, semantic integration, and methods that combine symbolic knowledge representation with neural learning.

My background spans more than fifteen years in academic research, software engineering, and technical leadership, including roles as a CTO and IT architect. This experience informs my approach to research problems involving heterogeneous data, from organizing and representing knowledge to applying it in practical settings.

My wider interests include distributed computing and high-performance computing.
